# Channel Notes

A private scratchpad per channel, opened from a button in the channel header. Notes are stored locally and never sent anywhere. Doubles as the worked example of the BetterSlack widget API.

- A button in the channel header opens a scratchpad for that conversation, and only that one.
- Notes are kept by the loader, in your BetterSlack settings file — not in Slack, not in localStorage, and never sent anywhere.
- It is also the worked example for the widget API: modal, confirm, toast and a persisted field, in about a hundred readable lines.
