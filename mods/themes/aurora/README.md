# Aurora

Frosted glass over a slow aurora gradient. Translucent panels, soft 14px corners, a violet glow that follows what you hover. Heavier on the GPU than a flat theme.

- A slow violet-to-teal gradient sits behind the whole client, and every panel above it is frosted glass rather than a flat fill.
- Panels, menus and dialogs are translucent, with 14px corners and no hard borders.
- What you hover picks up a violet glow, so the pointer is always findable.
- Heavier than a flat theme: the blur is real, and on an older machine it costs frames.
