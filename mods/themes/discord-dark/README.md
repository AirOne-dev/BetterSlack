# Discord Dark

Slack rebuilt as Discord: colours sampled from the real client, round server icons with the sliding pill, and Discord's sidebar. Brings in the member column and account strip as plugins.

- Colours are sampled from the real Discord client rather than guessed: the same greys, the same blurple.
- The workspace rail becomes Discord’s server list — round icons, and the white pill that slides to whatever is selected.
- It asks to switch on Member Sidebar and Sidebar Account Strip, which are what put a member column on the right and your own face at the bottom left. Decline and you still get the theme.
